How a same-day crown works.

No temporary crown, no second appointment, no impression tray. We scan your tooth, design the crown, and mill it here while you wait.

  • CEREC same-day crowns and bridges, made in the clinic
  • PrimeScan digital impressions instead of a mouthful of putty
  • 3D imaging on site for implants, wisdom teeth, and root canals
Scan

A small camera maps the tooth in a couple of minutes. No putty, no gagging.

Design

Your dentist shapes the crown on screen to fit your bite exactly.

Mill

A porcelain block is cut to that design here in the clinic while you wait.

Fit

The finished crown is bonded the same day. You leave done.

Implants, dentures, and bridges

Implants and the surgery behind them

Dr. Walter Herrador handles implants here, along with the bone and gum surgery that sometimes has to come first. He trained further at the Pacific Implant Institute, UBC, UCLA, and McGill after dental school, on top of wisdom teeth and root canals.

Dentures, partials, and bridges

Full and partial dentures, fixed bridges, and implants for people who want something that doesn't come out. Fitted here and adjusted until they're right.

If you snore or wake up exhausted, ask about sleep apnea treatment. Dr. Herrador treats it here too, alongside implants and extractions.

Braces and aligners

Braces, Invisalign, SureSmile, and early treatment, all here in Steinbach. No drive to Winnipeg.

Questions people ask

Do you take new patients?

Yes. Steinbach keeps growing and we're always taking on new families.

Do I need a referral for braces or implants?

No. Orthodontics and implants are handled right here, no referral to a specialist required.

What does a first visit include?

Digital x-rays, a full exam, and a cleaning. We'll show you what we see on the screen and answer every question before you leave.

Do you accept my insurance?

We accept most Canadian dental insurance programs, and our staff prepare the forms for you. Payment is due at the time of service, and our fees are the same whether or not you're insured.

Are you open Saturdays?

Yes, 8:00 to 4:00.
